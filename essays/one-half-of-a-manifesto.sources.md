# Sourcing appendix: One-Half of a Manifesto (2000)

Status of every figure added to the essay on Oct 23, 2026, as checked by independent web searches the same day. Interim: 5 of 11 claim clusters complete. Statuses are evidence for the editor to judge, not rulings.

Legend. **Confirmed**: the claim as written matches the source. **Flagged**: the checker proposes different wording; the evidence is shown so the editor can decide. **Unverified**: no source found either way. Source grade: P = primary (agency, filing, court, company report), S = reputable secondary (wire service, major outlet), A = aggregator or community source.
