# Jaron Lanier: Appearances and Citations, 2018 to October 2026

**Scope.** From the launch of Ten Arguments for Deleting Your Social Media Accounts Right Now (May 2018) through October 23, 2026. Three parts: what Lanier published (primary), where he appeared (talks, broadcast, podcasts, film), and what others wrote about him (secondary).

**Limits.** Compiled from Lanier's own site listings, podcast directories, venue and outlet pages, and news coverage. Lanier gives dozens of talks a year; this is the verifiable core, not a census. "≈" means the date comes from a single listing. "Unverified" means one weak source. No new book has been announced since 2018.

## Part 3. Secondary: what others wrote about Lanier, 2018 to date

- Franklin Foer, The New York Times Book Review, June 2018. Foer deleted his Facebook account after reading it.
- Mark O'Connell, The New Yorker, September 2018.
- Publishers Weekly (critical: "tediously reiterates well-known pitfalls"). Kirkus Reviews ("a useful experiment"). Fast Company. National Review.

### Academic and policy citations
- Posner and Weyl, Radical Markets (Princeton, 2018), chapter 5, "Data as Labor": the general-audience version of the Who Owns the Future? argument and the 2018 AEA paper.
- The 2018 AEA paper is the most-cited formal statement of Lanier's data-as-labor idea. Julian Jonker's "Is Data Labor?" (Business Ethics Quarterly) is a direct response.
- RadicalxChange (founded by Weyl, 2018) carries data dignity as a program; Lanier spoke at its 2019 and 2020 events.
- Shoshana Zuboff, The Age of Surveillance Capitalism (2019), describes the same extraction. A direct citation of Lanier was not verified here.
- For a full count, Google Scholar lists Ten Arguments and Who Owns the Future? with thousands of citations each. Searching "Lanier" with "data dignity" or "siren servers" returns the current literature.
